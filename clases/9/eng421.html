📍 Prepositions of Place

Unit 6 - Describing Where Things Are

Learn to describe the location of objects and people using prepositions

📖 Theory

What are Prepositions of Place?

Prepositions of place are words that tell us where something or someone is located. They describe the position or location of nouns in relation to other nouns.

Key Points:

  • Prepositions of place answer the question: "Where?"
  • They are used with the verb "to be" (is, are, am) or action verbs
  • They connect a noun to its location
  • Common prepositions: in, on, under, behind, in front of, next to, between, above, below, near

How to Write Sentences with Prepositions of Place:

Sentence Structure:

Subject + Verb (to be) + Preposition + Object/Place
Example: The book is on the table.
Example: The cat is under the chair.

Important Rules:

  • IN = inside something (enclosed space)
  • ON = touching the surface of something
  • AT = specific point or location
  • Use "is" for singular subjects (he, she, it, the book)
  • Use "are" for plural subjects (they, we, the books)

Examples

Visual Examples:

📚 📦
The book is IN the box.
📱 🪑
The phone is ON the chair.
🪑
The shoe is UNDER the chair.
🌳 🏠
The tree is BEHIND the house.

Sentence Examples:

The cat is on the table.
El gato está sobre la mesa.
The keys are in my bag.
Las llaves están en mi bolso.
The ball is under the bed.
La pelota está debajo de la cama.
The car is behind the house.
El carro está detrás de la casa.
The school is in front of the park.
La escuela está frente al parque.
The bank is next to the supermarket.
El banco está al lado del supermercado.
The library is between the school and the hospital.
La biblioteca está entre la escuela y el hospital.
The picture is above the sofa.
El cuadro está arriba del sofá.

More Examples with Different Verbs:

  • I put the book on the shelf.
  • She sits next to her friend.
  • They live near the beach.
  • He stands in front of the class.
