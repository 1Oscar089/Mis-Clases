How Often?

Daily Activities & Frequency Adverbs

Learn to ask and answer questions about how frequently you do activities

📖 Theory

Questions with "How often...?"

Questions with "How often" ask about the frequency of an activity - how frequent or how many times something is done.

Question Structure:

How often + do/does + subject + verb + complement?

Examples:

How often do you clean your house?

How often does she exercise?

Important Rules:

  • Use "do" with: I, you, we, they
  • Use "does" with: he, she, it
  • The verb stays in base form (no -s ending)
  • Answers use frequency adverbs: always, usually, often, sometimes, hardly ever, never

Frequency Adverbs Scale:

100% Always Siempre - all the time
80% Usually Usualmente - most of the time
60% Often A menudo - many times
40% Sometimes A veces - occasionally
10% Hardly ever Casi nunca - rarely
0% Never Nunca - not at all

Answer Structure:

Subject + frequency adverb + verb (conjugated) + complement

Examples:

I always clean the house.

He usually cleans the house.

💡 Examples

Common Daily Activities:

🏠 clean the house
🏃 exercise
📚 study English
play video games
check my phone
🍳 cook dinner

Question & Answer Examples:

Q: How often do you clean your house?
A: I always clean the house.
P: ¿Con qué frecuencia limpias tu casa? R: Siempre limpio la casa.
Q: How often does he clean the house?
A: He usually cleans the house.
P: ¿Con qué frecuencia limpia él la casa? R: Él usualmente limpia la casa.
Q: How often do you exercise?
A: I often exercise in the morning.
P: ¿Con qué frecuencia haces ejercicio? R: A menudo hago ejercicio en la mañana.
Q: How often does she study English?
A: She sometimes studies English.
P: ¿Con qué frecuencia estudia ella inglés? R: Ella a veces estudia inglés.
Q: How often do they play video games?
A: They hardly ever play video games.
P: ¿Con qué frecuencia juegan ellos videojuegos? R: Ellos casi nunca juegan videojuegos.
Q: How often do you cook dinner?
A: I never cook dinner.
P: ¿Con qué frecuencia cocinas la cena? R: Nunca cocino la cena.

Remember:

  • With he/she/it, add -s to the verb in answers: "He cleans", "She studies"
  • In questions, use base form: "How often does he clean?" (NOT "cleans")
  • Frequency adverbs go before the main verb
