📖 Theory

Negative Sentences in Simple Present

We use negative sentences to say what we don't do or what doesn't happen regularly.

Negative Sentence Structure:

Subject + don't / doesn't + verb (base form) + complement

Examples:

✅ I don't take a shower in the morning.

✅ You don't go to school on Sundays.

✅ We don't play soccer in the evening.

✅ They don't watch TV in the afternoon.

Yes/No Question Structure:

Do / Does + subject + verb (base form) + complement + ?

Examples:

✅ Do you go to school on Sundays?

✅ Do we play soccer in the evening?

✅ Do they watch TV in the afternoon?

Important Rules:

  • don't = do not → use with: I, you, we, they
  • doesn't = does not → use with: he, she, it
  • The verb stays in base form (NO -s ending) in negatives and questions
  • Use Do for questions with: I, you, we, they
  • Use Does for questions with: he, she, it

Short Answers:

Question Yes ✅ No ❌
Do you go to school on Sundays? Yes, I do. No, I don't.
Do we play soccer in the evening? Yes, we do. No, we don't.
Do they watch TV in the afternoon? Yes, they do. No, they don't.

Remember:

  • In negative sentences: don't/doesn't + base verb (NOT "doesn't plays" ❌)
  • In questions: Do/Does + subject + base verb (NOT "Does she plays" ❌)
  • Short answers use do/don't or does/doesn't
  • Always use appropriate punctuation: . for statements, ? for questions

Negative Sentence Examples:

I don't take a shower in the morning.
Yo no me baño en la mañana.
You don't go to school on Sundays.
Tú no vas a la escuela los domingos.
We don't play soccer in the evening.
Nosotros no jugamos fútbol en la tarde/noche.
They don't watch TV in the afternoon.
Ellos no ven televisión en la tarde.

Yes/No Question Examples:

Do you go to school on Sundays?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
P: ¿Vas a la escuela los domingos? R: Sí, voy. / No, no voy.
Do we play soccer in the evening?
Yes, we do. / No, we don't.
P: ¿Jugamos fútbol en la tarde? R: Sí, jugamos. / No, no jugamos.
Do they watch TV in the afternoon?
Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
P: ¿Ellos ven televisión en la tarde? R: Sí, ven. / No, no ven.
Do you brush your teeth in the morning?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
P: ¿Te cepillas los dientes en la mañana? R: Sí, me cepillo. / No, no me cepillo.

Key Differences - Affirmative vs Negative vs Question:

  • ✅ Affirmative: You watch TV in the afternoon.
  • ❌ Negative: You don't watch TV in the afternoon.
  • ❓ Question: Do you watch TV in the afternoon?
