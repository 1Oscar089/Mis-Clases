🗓️ Simple Present Tense

Daily Routines & Time Expressions

Learn to talk about your daily activities using the Simple Present Tense

📖 Theory

What is the Simple Present Tense?

The Simple Present Tense is used to talk about habits, routines, and daily activities that happen regularly.

Affirmative Sentence Structure:

Subject + Verb (base form / +s) + Complement + Time Expression

Examples:

✅ I take a shower in the morning.

✅ She watches TV in the evening.

✅ They go to school on weekdays.

Important Rules:

  • I / You / We / They → verb in base form (no -s)
  • He / She / It → verb + s or es
  • Time expressions tell us when the activity happens
  • Use "in" for: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
  • Use "at" for: at night
  • Use "on" for: on weekdays, on weekends

💡 Examples

I take a shower in the morning.
Yo me baño en la mañana.
We go to school on weekdays.
Nosotros vamos a la escuela entre semana.
You watch TV in the evening.
Tú ves televisión en la tarde/noche.
I go to bed at night.
Yo me voy a la cama en la noche.
She helps with the housework in the afternoon.
Ella ayuda con el quehacer en la tarde.
He does his homework in the afternoon.
Él hace su tarea en la tarde.

Remember:

  • With he/she/it: add -s or -es to the verb
  • watch → watches, do → does, go → goes, have → has
  • Time expressions usually go at the end of the sentence
