🌅 Parts of the Day

Unit 5 - Simple Present Tense (Affirmative Sentences)

Learn to describe daily routines and activities using time expressions

📖 Theory

Parts of the Day

A day is normally divided into four parts:

in the morning
From sunrise to 12:00 p.m.
☀️
in the afternoon
12:00 p.m. - 6:00 p.m.
🌆
in the evening
6:00 p.m. - 10:00 p.m.
🌙
at night
10:00 p.m. - sunrise

Important Grammar Rules:

  • We use the preposition "in" for: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening
  • We use the preposition "at" for: at night
  • These expressions are used with the Simple Present Tense to describe daily routines
  • The Simple Present is used for habits, routines, and general truths

Simple Present Tense Structure:

Affirmative Sentences:

  • I / You / We / They + verb (base form)
  • He / She / It + verb + s/es

Example: She works in the morning. / They study in the afternoon.

Examples

I take a shower in the morning.
Yo me baño en la mañana.
We watch TV in the afternoon.
Nosotros vemos televisión en la tarde.
They play soccer in the evening.
Ellos juegan fútbol en la noche (tarde).
She reads a book at night.
Ella lee un libro en la noche.
He goes to work in the morning.
Él va a trabajar en la mañana.
We have dinner in the evening.
Nosotros cenamos en la tarde/noche.
