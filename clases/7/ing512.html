📖 Theory

What are the Days of the Week?

In English, the week consists of seven days. These days are used to organize our time and schedule activities.

Important Rules:

  • Days of the week are always capitalized in English (Monday, Tuesday, etc.)
  • We use the preposition "on" with days: "on Monday", "on Friday"
  • The week starts on Sunday or Monday depending on the country
  • Weekdays: Monday to Friday (work/school days)
  • Weekend: Saturday and Sunday (rest days)

Abbreviations:

Days can be abbreviated: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun

Vocabulary

Monday First weekday
Tuesday Second weekday
Wednesday Middle of week
Thursday Fourth weekday
Friday Last weekday
Saturday First weekend day
Sunday Last day of week

Related Words:

Weekday - Monday to Friday

Weekend - Saturday and Sunday

Today - The current day

Tomorrow - The next day

Yesterday - The previous day

💡 Examples

1. Today is Monday.

Hoy es lunes.

2. I go to school on Tuesday and Thursday.

Voy a la escuela los martes y jueves.

3. What day is tomorrow?

¿Qué día es mañana?

4. We don't work on weekends.

No trabajamos los fines de semana.

5. My birthday is on Friday.

Mi cumpleaños es el viernes.

6. Wednesday is the middle of the week.

El miércoles es la mitad de la semana.
