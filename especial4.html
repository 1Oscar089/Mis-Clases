🗺️ Giving Directions

Unit 4 - How to Get There

Learn to give and follow directions using turn left, turn right, continue straight and more

📖 Theory

Direction Vocabulary

We use these phrases when giving directions to a specific place:

↩️ turn left
↪️ turn right
⬆️ continue straight
⬇️ go back (2) blocks
️↩️ take the second left
↪️↪️ take the second right
🔵 take the first exit on the roundabout

Important Rules:

  • Use imperative form (base verb): "Turn left", "Go straight", NOT "You turn left"
  • Use "on the" for streets: "on the second street", "on the first corner"
  • Use "at the" for specific points: "at the traffic light", "at the roundabout"
  • Always start with the action verb: Turn, Go, Continue, Take

💡 Examples

Turn left on the first street.
Dobla a la izquierda en la primera calle.
Turn right on the second corner.
Dobla a la derecha en la segunda esquina.
Continue straight for two blocks.
Continúa derecho por dos cuadras.
Go back two blocks and turn left.
Regresa dos cuadras y dobla a la izquierda.
Take the second right on the roundabout.
Toma la segunda salida en la rotonda.
Take the first exit on the roundabout.
Toma la primera salida en la rotonda.

Complete Direction Example:

"Excuse me, how do I get to the library?"

  • 1. Continue straight for one block.
  • 2. Turn right on the second street.
  • 3. Continue straight for two blocks.
  • 4. The library is on the left.
