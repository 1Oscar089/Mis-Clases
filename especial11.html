📖 Theory: How to Write Dates

Date Format in English:

  • Month + Day (ordinal) + Year: September 15th, 1821
  • Day (ordinal) + of + Month + Year: The 15th of September, 1821
  • In American English: Month Day, Year → September 15, 1821
  • In British English: Day Month Year → 15 September 1821

Months of the Year:

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December

💡 Remember: Months always start with a capital letter!

Ordinal Numbers for Days:

  • 1st (first), 2nd (second), 3rd (third)
  • 4th-20th: fourth, fifth, sixth... twentieth
  • 21st (twenty-first), 22nd (twenty-second), 23rd (twenty-third)
  • 30th (thirtieth), 31st (thirty-first)
💡 Accepted formats for this practice:
• "September 15th" / "September 15" / "15th of September"
• "the fifteenth of September"
• "September the fifteenth"
• For years: "eighteen twenty-one" or "1821"
