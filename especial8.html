📖 Theory: How to Spell Numbers

Basic Rules:

  • 0-12: Have unique names (zero, one, two, three... twelve).
  • 13-19: End in -teen (thirteen, fourteen... nineteen).
  • 20, 30, 40...: End in -ty (twenty, thirty, forty... ninety).
  • 21-99 (Compound numbers): Use a hyphen (-) between the tens and the units.
    Example: 21 = twenty-one, 45 = forty-five, 99 = ninety-nine.
  • 100: one hundred.
