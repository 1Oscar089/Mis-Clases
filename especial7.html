🔢 Unit 5: Ordinal Numbers & Birthdays

8th Grade - Dates, Ordinals & Family Birthdays

Learn to write ordinal numbers and ask about family birthdays

📖 Theory

What are Ordinal Numbers?

Ordinal numbers tell us the position or order of something in a sequence. They are essential for writing dates, rankings, and birthdays.

🎯 The Pattern of Endings:

1, 21, 31 → st
2, 22 → nd
3, 23 → rd
4-20, 24-30 → th

⚠️ Exceptions: 11th, 12th, 13th (NOT 11st, 12nd, 13rd)

Asking About Birthdays:

  • Question: "When is your [relative]'s birthday?"
  • Answer: "It's on [Month] [ordinal date]." or "My [relative]'s birthday is on [Month] [ordinal date]."
  • Use the preposition "on" with specific dates
  • Use the possessive 's for family members: mom's, dad's, sister's, brother's
📅 Months of the Year: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December

Examples

Ordinal Numbers in Dates:

My birthday is on March 15th (fifteenth).
Mi cumpleaños es el 15 de marzo.
Her birthday is on July 4th (fourth).
Su cumpleaños es el 4 de julio.
Today is the 23rd (twenty-third) of September.
Hoy es 23 de septiembre.

Birthday Questions & Answers:

Q: When is your mom's birthday?
A: It's on May 21st. / My mom's birthday is on May twenty-first.
P: ¿Cuándo es el cumpleaños de tu mamá? R: Es el 21 de mayo.
Q: When is your dad's birthday?
A: It's on December 3rd.
P: ¿Cuándo es el cumpleaños de tu papá? R: Es el 3 de diciembre.
Q: When is your sister's birthday?
A: Her birthday is on August 17th.
P: ¿Cuándo es el cumpleaños de tu hermana? R: Su cumpleaños es el 17 de agosto.

Family Members Vocabulary:

  • mom / mother → mom's birthday
  • dad / father → dad's birthday
  • sister → sister's birthday
  • brother → brother's birthday
  • grandmother / grandma → grandmother's birthday
  • grandfather / grandpa → grandfather's birthday
  • uncle → uncle's birthday
  • aunt → aunt's birthday
  • cousin → cousin's birthday
