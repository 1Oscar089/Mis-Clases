Theory

The Months of the Year

There are 12 months in a year. Each month has a different number of days.

Important Rules:

  • Months of the year always start with a capital letter: January, February, March...
  • We use the preposition "in" with months: in January, in March, in December
  • Each month can be abbreviated: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
  • The year starts in January and ends in December

All 12 Months:

Days in Each Month:

  • 31 days: January, March, May, July, August, October, December
  • 30 days: April, June, September, November
  • 28 or 29 days: February (29 in leap years)
