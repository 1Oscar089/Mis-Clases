🔢 Ordinal Numbers & Dates

Unit 5 - First, Second, Third... and How to Say Dates

Learn ordinal numbers from 1st to 31st and how to read dates in American English

📖 Theory

What are Ordinal Numbers?

Ordinal numbers tell us the position or order of something in a sequence: first, second, third, etc. They are used for dates, rankings, floors of buildings, and more.

📋 Pattern of Endings:

1 st
2 nd
3 rd
4-20 th
21 st
22 nd
23 rd
24-30 th
31 st
First = 1st

🎯 Remember the Pattern:

1, 21, 31 → st
2, 22 → nd
3, 23 → rd
4-20, 24-30 → th

️ Exceptions: 11th, 12th, 13th (NOT 11st, 12nd, 13rd)

Complete List of Ordinal Numbers (1st - 31st):

First = 1st
Second = 2nd
Third = 3rd
Fourth = 4th
Fifth = 5th
Sixth = 6th
Seventh = 7th
Eighth = 8th
Ninth = 9th
Tenth = 10th
Eleventh = 11th
Twelfth = 12th
Thirteenth = 13th
Twentieth = 20th
Twenty-first = 21st
Twenty-second = 22nd
Twenty-third = 23rd
Thirtieth = 30th
Thirty-first = 31st

Important Rules:

  • Ordinal numbers end in st, nd, rd, or th
  • Use st for numbers ending in 1 (except 11): 1st, 21st, 31st
  • Use nd for numbers ending in 2 (except 12): 2nd, 22nd
  • Use rd for numbers ending in 3 (except 13): 3rd, 23rd
  • Use th for all others: 4th, 5th, 11th, 12th, 13th, 20th...
  • Special spellings: first (not oneth), second (not twoth), third (not threeth), fifth (not fiveth), ninth (not nineth), twelfth (not twelveth)

How to Read Dates in American English:

Date Format: MM/DD (Month/Day)

  • In American English, the month comes first, then the day
  • The day is read as an ordinal number
  • Format: Month + ordinal day
  • Example: 09/03 → September third (NOT September three)
📅 Months of the Year: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December

Remember:

  • 09/03 in American English = September third (month first!)
  • The day is ALWAYS an ordinal number: third, NOT three
  • Write dates as: Month + day (no "the" or "of" needed in American English)

Activity 3 When is Your Birthday? 📓 Notebook Activity

Instructions: This activity is to be completed in your notebook. Follow the steps below and fill in the chart by asking your classmates about their birthdays.

📓 Notebook Activity: Birthday Survey

Key Phrases:

Question: When is your birthday?
Answer: My birthday is on May 21st. / It's on _____ (month) _____ (date).

📋 Steps to Follow:

  1. Copy the following chart in your notebook:
Classmate 1 Classmate 2 Classmate 3 Classmate 4 Classmate 5
(write name & birthday) (write name & birthday) (write name & birthday) (write name & birthday) (write name & birthday)
  1. Stand up and ask five different classmates: "When is your birthday?"
  2. Your classmate must answer: "It's on _____ (month) _____ (date)."
  3. Write their name and birthday in your chart.
  4. After finishing, be ready to answer: "When is [classmate's name]'s birthday?"

💡 Example:

You: When is your birthday, Carlos?

Carlos: It's on March fifteenth.

You write in chart: Classmate 1: Carlos - March 15th

✅ Remember:
  • Always use ordinal numbers for dates: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th...
  • Use the preposition "on" with dates: "My birthday is on May 21st"
  • Months always start with a capital letter
