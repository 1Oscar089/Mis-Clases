Important Rules:

  • Use imperative form (base verb): "Turn left", "Go straight"
  • Use "on" for streets: "on Los Amates Avenue", "on Rafael Cabrera Street"
  • Use "for" with blocks: "for two blocks", "for three blocks"
  • Use "next to", "between", "on the corner of" for final location
  • Always start from your current position

Example Directions:

Turn right on Rafael Cabrera Street.
Dobla a la derecha en la calle Rafael Cabrera.
Then turn left on Los Amates Avenue.
Luego dobla a la izquierda en la avenida Los Amates.
Continue straight for two blocks.
Continúa derecho por dos cuadras.
The factory is next to the mall.
La fábrica está al lado del centro comercial.
