🏆 Superlative Adjectives

9th Grade - The Highest, The Biggest, The Most Beautiful

Learn to describe the extreme qualities of places and things

📖 Theory: Spelling Rules for Superlative Adjectives

Superlative adjectives are used to describe the highest degree of a quality. They answer the question: "Which one is the most/least...?"

Rule #1: One-syllable adjectives

Add -est to most one-syllable adjectives.
new → newest
old → oldest

Rule #2: One-syllable ending in -e

Add -st to one-syllable adjectives ending in -e.
safe → safest
wide → widest

Rule #3: CVC pattern (consonant-vowel-consonant)

Double the last consonant and add -est.
big → biggest
hot → hottest

Rule #4: Two-syllable ending in -y

Drop the -y and add -iest.
lovely → loveliest
sunny → sunniest

Rule #5: Two or more syllables

Add most before the adjective.
interesting → most interesting
beautiful → most beautiful

Exceptions: Irregular adjectives

Some adjectives have irregular superlative forms.
good → best
bad → worst

Important Rules:

  • Always use "the" before superlative adjectives: "the biggest", "the most beautiful"
  • Superlatives compare three or more things
  • Use "in" for places: "the tallest building in San Salvador"
  • Use "of" for groups: "the smartest student of the class"

🇸🇻 Examples: Superlatives with Salvadoran Places

🏔️ El Cerro El Pital is the highest mountain in El Salvador.
El Cerro El Pital es la montaña más alta de El Salvador.
Lago de Coatepeque is the most beautiful lake in the country.
El Lago de Coatepeque es el lago más hermoso del país.
Volcán de Santa Ana is the biggest volcano in El Salvador.
El Volcán de Santa Ana es el volcán más grande de El Salvador.
🏛️ Joya de Cerén is the most interesting archaeological site in Central America.
Joya de Cerén es el sitio arqueológico más interesante de Centroamérica.
🏖️ Playa El Tunco has the best waves for surfing.
Playa El Tunco tiene las mejores olas para surfear.
🌆 San Salvador is the largest city in El Salvador.
San Salvador es la ciudad más grande de El Salvador.
🌺 Flor de Izote is the most beautiful flower in the country.
La Flor de Izote es la flor más hermosa del país.
🦜 Torogoz is the most colorful bird in El Salvador.
El Torogoz es el pájaro más colorido de El Salvador.

Remember:

  • the highest = el más alto (one syllable: high + est)
  • the most beautiful = el más hermoso (three syllables: most + beautiful)
  • the biggest = el más grande (CVC: big + g + est)
  • the best = el mejor (irregular: good → best)
