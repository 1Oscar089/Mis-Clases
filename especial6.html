️ Unit 4: Getting Around Town

9th Grade - Giving Directions & Places in Town

Learn to navigate the city and describe locations

Vocabulary: Places in Town

  • Hospital: A place for medical treatment. 🏥
  • Bank: A place to keep money. 🏦
  • School: A place for education. 🏫
  • Police Station: Headquarters for police. 👮
  • Fire Station: Headquarters for firefighters. 🚒
  • Theater: A place for movies or plays. 🎭
  • Local Market: A place to buy food and goods. 🛒
  • Mail / Post Office: A place to send letters. ️
  • Gas Station: A place to get fuel for cars. ⛽
  • Super Market: A large grocery store. 🛍️
  • Park: A green area for relaxation. 🌳

Direction Phrases:

  • Turn left / Turn right: Change direction.
  • Go straight / Continue straight: Keep moving forward.
  • Next to: Beside something.
  • Between: In the middle of two things.
  • On the corner of: At the intersection.
