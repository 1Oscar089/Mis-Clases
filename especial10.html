📖 Theory: How to Tell Time

Basic Structure:

  • "It's [hour] o'clock." → for exact hours (3:00 = It's three o'clock)
  • "It's [hour] [minutes]." → 3:15 = It's three fifteen
  • "It's [minutes] past [hour]." → 3:15 = It's a quarter past three
  • "It's [minutes] to [next hour]." → 3:45 = It's a quarter to four
  • "It's half past [hour]." → 3:30 = It's half past three
📋 Key Vocabulary:
  • o'clock = en punto
  • a quarter past = y cuarto (15 min)
  • half past = y media (30 min)
  • a quarter to = menos cuarto (45 min)
  • past = después de (1-30 min)
  • to = para / menos (31-59 min)

Examples:

  • 3:00 → It's three o'clock.
  • 3:15 → It's three fifteen. or It's a quarter past three.
  • 3:30 → It's three thirty. or It's half past three.
  • 3:45 → It's three forty-five. or It's a quarter to four.
  • 3:20 → It's three twenty. or It's twenty past three.
